Guverte Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 13, 2026

This policy explains how the Guverte mobile game handles player data, advertising, purchases, diagnostics, and optional character creation features.

1. Local Game Data

Guverte may store game progress, save slots, career state, language, sound, route, ship, character, premium access, and gameplay preferences on the player's device. In-game phone, family chat, crew chat, and AI Mate messages are simulation features and are not real messaging services.

2. Camera and Reference Photo

Players may optionally upload or capture a reference photo for character creation. The image is used to help create or suggest an in-game character preview. Unless a separate online feature is clearly enabled, this reference image is not uploaded to a developer server. Denying camera or photo permission does not block the main game.

3. Advertising

The free version of Guverte may show interstitial ads at natural breaks, such as month-end, contract-end, or training-center transitions. Ads are provided through Google Mobile Ads. Google and its advertising partners may process device information, advertising ID, IP address, approximate location, app interaction, diagnostics, and similar data for ad delivery, measurement, frequency capping, fraud prevention, and legal compliance.

Ads are not intended to interrupt critical emergency or timed gameplay moments. If the player purchases the remove-ads product, the game stops requesting and showing ads.

Google privacy resources: Google Privacy Policy and Google partner sites policy.

4. Consent and Privacy Choices

Where required, Guverte uses Google User Messaging Platform (UMP) to show consent or privacy choice forms before ad requests. Players can reopen ad privacy choices from the game's options or phone/settings area when the Android build supports it.

5. Purchases

Premium content and remove-ads products are handled through Google Play Billing. Guverte does not store card numbers, bank details, or Google Play passwords. The app may process purchase status, product IDs, and purchase tokens only to unlock access and restore purchases.

6. Data Sharing

The developer does not sell player data. Data may be processed by third parties only for the following purposes:

  • Google Mobile Ads and UMP for ads, consent, and measurement.
  • Google Play Billing for purchases and restore checks.
  • Legal requests where required by applicable law.

7. Diagnostics and Tester Reports

The app may keep limited local diagnostics such as JavaScript errors, startup health, freeze detection, device/screen summary, and save health. These diagnostics are not automatically sent to the developer. They are shared only when the player chooses to copy, export, or submit a tester report.

8. Data Retention and Deletion

Local game data remains on the device until the player clears saves, clears Android app data, or uninstalls the app. Data processed by Google services is handled according to Google's own retention policies.

9. Children's Privacy

Guverte is a maritime simulation game for a general audience. The game does not knowingly request direct personal details from children. Store target audience, advertising, and family settings must be configured correctly for each release region.

10. Security

Android backup is disabled for the app and cleartext network traffic is not allowed. No technical method can guarantee absolute security.
