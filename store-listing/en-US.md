# Guverte: Maritime Career Simulator

## Short description

Stand watch, use Radar/ECDIS/VHF, and face realistic maritime decisions.

## Full description

Guverte is a decision-driven maritime career simulator that follows your journey from cadet to master.

Sail realistic trade routes, stand bridge watches, and practice with Radar, ARPA, ECDIS, AIS, VHF, and GMDSS. Your choices in pilotage, mooring, fire response, engine alarms, COLREG, cargo operations, and inspections shape your career and crew relationships.

- Live voyages with waypoints, CPA/TCPA, UKC, and weather
- Container, tanker, bulk, and specialist ship operations
- Interactive chart and device training center
- Manual saves, automatic recovery, export, and import
- Turkish, English, Spanish, German, French, Russian, and Chinese
- Specialist vessels and advanced operations in the Premium pack

Guverte is a learning support game. It does not replace approved maritime training or vessel procedures.
